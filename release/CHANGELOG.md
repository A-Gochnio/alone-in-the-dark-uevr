# Changelog

## 0.2.0-beta (2026-10-26)

Public beta. Not yet played through to the end of the game in VR.

- Telescope: the view switches to a flat virtual screen while you look through it (its lens effects flickered in 3D).
- Deleting saves is disabled: Y does nothing on the Load Game screens, so a stray press can't delete a save.
- Puzzles, menus, documents and the telescope were checked with simulated Quest controller input (dial safe and padlock solved, menu tabs, document pages and view, item picker).

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-26)

First public release.

- First person with 6DOF head tracking, eye-height camera, crouch lowers the view.
- All guns attach to the right hand and shoot along the barrel; red laser aim dot.
- Two-handed shotgun and Tommy gun (left grip near the barrel).
- Comfortable VR layout: right grip aims, right trigger fires, left trigger melee, X reload / hold stealth, Y inventory / hold pause, left grip + right stick for weapons and items.
- Menus fixed in the room, smaller head-following HUD, no head-locked crosshair.
- Close-up puzzles get the plain controller layout.
- Works for Edward and Emily. Not yet tested through to the end of the game.
- Install.bat: installs the profile and Engine.ini tweaks, with backups and -Uninstall.
- Recommended settings for a steady 90 FPS on an RTX 4070 Ti (Quest 3, Virtual Desktop).
